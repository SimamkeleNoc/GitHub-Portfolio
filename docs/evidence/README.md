# GuardPulse — Portfolio Evidence

This folder contains screenshots of the GuardPulse online store, developed independently as my main project evidence for PRP370S.

## Evidence 1: Homepage

**File:** `01-guardpulse-homepage.png`

**What it demonstrates:** The GuardPulse storefront, navigation, branding, and customer-facing design for a South African home-security store.

**Reflection:** This demonstrates how I presented the purpose of the application to customers and organised the main navigation to make product categories accessible.

## Evidence 2: Product Catalogue

**File:** `02-guardpulse-product-catalogue.png`

**What it demonstrates:** Product cards with product images, names, descriptions, and prices.

**Reflection:** This shows the importance of presenting product information clearly so customers can compare security products and decide what to purchase.

## Evidence 3: Shopping Cart

**File:** `03-guardpulse-cart.png`

**What it demonstrates:** A selected product, quantity controls, and the cart subtotal.

**Reflection:** The cart is an important part of the shopping journey. Its displayed quantities and totals need to remain consistent as customers change their selections.

## Evidence 4: Checkout

**File:** `04-guardpulse-checkout-redacted.png`

**What it demonstrates:** The checkout form, order summary, and estimated shipping cost.

**Reflection:** This illustrates how customer details, order information, and delivery costs are brought together before an order is submitted. The shipping estimate shown in the screenshot is an example, not a guarantee of the final delivery price.

## Evidence limitations

These screenshots provide visual evidence of the customer-facing website. They do not independently prove successful payment processing, email delivery, backend test coverage, or administrative functionality. Those claims require separate evidence where available.

The screenshots are included to support reflection on the development process and the customer experience. Customer-identifying information should not be published in portfolio evidence.
